# Data-led discovery, then later-window checks
Frozen 2026-10-09 01:29 Bangkok before later-window numbers are computed.
An initial 60-component NMF of earlier-half title TF-IDF was swamped by software. A second exploratory 160-component run removed generic/software terms (fixed seed 42, min document frequency 6, unigrams/bigrams). It surfaced physical/bio clusters: power/grid/data center (68/87), brain/cells/organoids (50), Nvidia/GPU/chips (62), space/NASA/telescope (118), SpaceX/moon/rocket (127), solar/energy/eclipse (158), and Chinese/robot/humanoid (84). The analyst inspected the discovery-half example titles, selected six physical/bio themes and narrowed ambiguous proxies. This is data-led human-assisted topic discovery, not an automatic objective ontology.
The earlier half is weeks July 27-August 25 inclusive (five complete weeks); test half August 31-October 4 (five complete weeks). No randomly mixed rows. The dataset is already known and this is not an independent preregistered external replication.
Six themes: data centers & grid, GPU/chip hardware, neural biology, aerospace, solar energy, physical robotics. Definitions in scripts/vcs_holdout.py. Remove software 'shared brain'/agent brains, eclipse/solar-system astronomy from solar energy, and ad/crawler robots from robotics. Regex imperfect; publish all definitions and discovery examples.
Two tests per theme (12 cards):
1) Persistence: later mean weekly share of eligible stories / earlier mean weekly share. >=1.5 strong support, >=0.8 weak support (not down more than 20%), <=0.5 refuted; otherwise inconclusive.
2) Later attention premium: later mean weekly points/story / later non-topic points/story. >=1.3 strong support, >=1.1 weak support, <=0.9 refuted; otherwise inconclusive.
Minimum of 20 stories in each half for persistence, and 20 in later half for premium. Below minimum: no card claiming support/refutation; blocked/inconclusive result is reported separately. No null->zero fabricated effect.
Robustness extra (if enough data): expanded discovered union premium after removal of top 1% by points; same premium thresholds. Discussion via actual descendants/story; same thresholds. Domain points outside leading 5 / inside >1.5 strong, >=1 weak, <=2/3 refuted. Domain isn't company.
A sustained attention signal is a research lead, not validation of technical results, revenue, startup eligibility, or a reason to contact a company. No mainstream-lead claim without an independent dated series. Lag in HN is only subsequent HN activity.
